Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Project Chess, we collect your email address, username, and password. We also collect gameplay data, puzzle attempts, and performance statistics to accurately calculate your Elo rating and provide a personalized experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to maintain your account, pair you with opponents, track your progress, and improve the overall quality of the platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement standard security measures, including password hashing, to protect your data. However, no electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use local storage to save your visual preferences (like dark/light theme) and session cookies to keep you logged in. You can clear these at any time via your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party APIs (such as Wikipedia for Grandmaster images or Groq for chess quotes). These services only receive the necessary request parameters and do not have access to your personal account data.

6. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
